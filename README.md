# BRICS Sports Expo Plan

GoBananas internal strategy for the sports conclave / expo under RFP clause 18 (m) of the BRICS Traditional & Indigenous Sports 2026 tender (Sports Authority of Gujarat).

**Live page:** https://akash-droid-dev.github.io/sports-expo-cl/

What the page covers:

- SportAccord exhibitor mix and what it means for this expo
- SFI 2026 recce findings: 14 build forms, zones and deltas
- The draft expo matrix corrected into a ten-zone floor (Z0–Z9) with an interactive plan
- 12 new exhibitor types, with evidence
- 310 tiered prospects (Indian, global-in-India, BRICS-nation and traditional-sport makers) in a searchable explorer
- Packages, visitor / exhibitor / delegation / lead flows, risks, a deck outline and sources

The page is a single self-contained `index.html`; its fonts load from Google Fonts. Floor areas and stall counts are planning figures, not quotations.
